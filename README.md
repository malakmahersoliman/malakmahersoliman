🌌 A Little About Me
I love figuring out how things work behind the scenes — then turning that curiosity into something people can actually use.
- 🧠 What excites me: backend engineering, thoughtful system design, and the why behind technical decisions.
- 🏦 Where my curiosity goes: banking & fintech — from transfers and payments to the systems that make them reliable.
- 🌱 What I'm learning: Java, Spring Boot, PostgreSQL, REST APIs, and the foundations of building dependable backends.
- 📱 Where I started: mobile apps, followed by hands-on full-stack work with .NET + Angular.
